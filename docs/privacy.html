Tricode Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

Tricode is an app by Szczepan Cieślik that creates and reads codes made of triangles. Tricode does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any of your data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting, and it does not send anything over the internet.

What the app uses on your device

Everything below is processed only on your device, and only when you use the feature.

  • Text you enter is turned into a code on your device.
  • Images you choose, paste or photograph are read to decode a code or to place a picture in the centre of one. They stay on your device and are never uploaded.
  • Camera (Android and iOS) is used only when you choose Take Photo, to photograph a code.
  • Photo library (Android and iOS) is used only to add a code when you choose Download. The app does not read your photos; images you pick are chosen through the system picker.
  • Clipboard is read or written only when you choose paste or copy.
  • Installed fonts (desktop) are listed so that you can pick one for text in a code.
  • Diagnostic logs stay on your device and do not contain the text of your codes.

Links in codes

If a code holds a link and you choose to open it, the link opens in your browser or another app on your device. That site or app has its own privacy policy. Tricode does not open links on its own.

App stores

The store you download Tricode from, such as Google Play or the App Store, may collect information such as download statistics under its own privacy policy. That information is not collected by Tricode.

Children

Tricode collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through GitHub issues.
